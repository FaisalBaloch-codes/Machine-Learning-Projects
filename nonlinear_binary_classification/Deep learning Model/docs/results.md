## Interpretation

The model performs very well: it correctly separates the two interleaving moon shapes and achieves high accuracy, precision, recall and AUC on data it never saw during training. This shows that the network genuinely learned the nonlinear pattern rather than memorizing the training data.

See `plots/06_confusion_matrix.png` for the breakdown of correct/incorrect
predictions, and `plots/07_decision_boundary.png` to visually confirm that
the network learned a **curved (nonlinear)** boundary rather than a straight
line.
